The problem

Someone browsing a listing has one immediate question about a property. For the sales team, that inquiry needs an owner, a follow-up date, and a record of what happened before the next call.

My work

I built the property platform and its CRM, including the REST APIs and protected React dashboards for administrators and sales agents.

Property discovery

Built searchable property listings, filters, and inquiry forms. The backend can associate the inquiry with the property that prompted it.

Sales operations

Designed lead distribution, pipeline stages, follow-up scheduling, and agent management. Administrators assign leads to active agents, who can update the records they own.

Reporting

Built React dashboards for property management, appointments, and conversion KPIs. The statistics endpoint calculates conversion from won leads and total leads in the relevant user’s scope.

Engineering decision

Whose pipeline should the dashboard count?

An agent needs a conversion rate for their own leads. An administrator needs the team view. In the statistics controller, the role determines the lead filter before the API counts both the total and the won leads. If there are no leads, the result is zero.

Outcome & evidence

The public property journey and the internal sales workflow share a lead record. The agent can see which listing prompted the inquiry, record the next follow-up, and move the lead through the pipeline.
