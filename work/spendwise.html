The problem

A list of transactions tells someone where their money went. To decide whether to spend again, they also need to know the monthly limit, how much is left, and when they first crossed a warning threshold.

My work

I built React dashboards and connected them to Express APIs through Axios for expense management, monthly budgets, and financial reports.

Manage expenses

Users can create, read, update, and delete expenses. The protected creation endpoint associates the amount and category with the signed-in user. The expense controller aggregates that user’s records for the current month.

Calculate the balance

The budget controller creates or updates a budget for a particular user, month, and year. Its status response includes the limit, spending, remainder, and percentage.

Surface a warning

The model stores the level of alert already sent. The expense controller checks the 80% warning and the 100% limit before returning a message to the interface.

Engineering decision

How do you warn someone at the right moment?

A calculated percentage describes the current balance. Remembering the previous alert level answers a different question: has this user already seen the warning for this budget? The budget record stores that state alongside the monthly amount.
